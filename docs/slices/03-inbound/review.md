- No High findings. The central controls hold:
  - Verified scope comes from one interceptor, and the argument resolver fails closed. `AgentRouteCoverageTest` scans every `@RestController`.
  - A lookup token is bound to one agent and one customer, and every failure returns the same 404.
  - The per-customer OTP and KBA limits are counted under the customer row lock.
  - OTP attempts are committed before the constant-time HMAC compare.
  - Reactivation transitions are guarded, and the user row is locked before the account row.
  - The outbox claim, sent and failed updates are guarded by `claimed_at`.
  - All new SQL uses named parameters, and sort keys go through a whitelist.
  - No codes, answers or full MSISDNs appear in logs or audit rows, and tests assert this.
